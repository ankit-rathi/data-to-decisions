# North Star

## Mission

Connect academic Data & AI knowledge with the practical realities of building and operating Data & AI systems.

## Positioning

Learn deeply. Connect theory with practice. Explain simply. Help students and professionals make better technical decisions.

## Core Question

> Where is there a meaningful gap between what academia teaches and what industry actually requires?

## Audience

- Data & AI students
- Early and mid-career professionals
- Experienced Data/AI engineers and architects

## Long-term Direction

Build credibility as a Data/AI educator and advisor.
