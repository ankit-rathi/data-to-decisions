Status: Example / starting topic

## Academia teaches

- Conditional probability
- Bayes' theorem
- Bayesian inference
- Priors, likelihood and posterior
- Base-rate reasoning

## Industry needs

- Base rates in real decision systems
- Evidence quality
- Calibration
- Changing distributions / base rates
- Probability versus decision
- Decision costs and thresholds
- Production monitoring

## The gap

The formula is usually easier to learn than the practical discipline of deciding whether the prior, evidence, assumptions and resulting probability are trustworthy enough to drive an action.

## Key practitioner insight

> Evidence updates belief. Base rates matter. Probability is not the decision.
