# Depth Review

Complete this after approximately the first 30 breadth topics.

The objective is **not** to pick the most viral topic.

The objective is to find the strongest intersection of:

> **Audience Pull × Personal Interest × Genuine Experience × Future Potential**

## Interpretation

### DOUBLE DOWN
Strong overall signal. Candidate for a deep series.

### DEVELOP
Promising but needs more experiments or audience evidence.

### MAINTAIN
Useful breadth content but not a primary focus.

### DROP
Low strategic value. Park it without guilt.

## Questions Before Going Deep

1. Can I say something genuinely useful?
2. Do I have enough experience to earn the audience's trust?
3. Do I enjoy spending substantial time on the subject?
4. Is there evidence that people want more?
5. Can I sustain the topic for 20–50 pieces without forcing it?
6. Does it strengthen the overall FDTD identity?

If the answer is mostly yes, go deeper.

## Important

A topic can be successful without becoming a business.

Likewise, a topic can have low initial views and still deserve development because it builds expertise, intellectual capital or future optionality.
