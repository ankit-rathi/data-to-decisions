# Philosophy

## The Central Idea

> **Data is not the destination. Better decisions and outcomes are.**

Most discussions begin with data, technology or tools.

From Data To Decisions begins with the **decision**.

```text
Decision
   ↑
Understanding
   ↑
Evidence / Data
   ↑
Question
   ↑
Real-world problem
```

The quality of a decision depends on more than data:

- context
- assumptions
- uncertainty
- incentives
- constraints
- judgement
- execution
- feedback

Therefore:

```text
DATA ≠ DECISION

DATA
  ↓
CONTEXT
  ↓
UNDERSTANDING
  ↓
JUDGEMENT
  ↓
DECISION
  ↓
ACTION
  ↓
OUTCOME
  ↓
FEEDBACK
```

## Why Breadth First?

The initial purpose of FDTD is to demonstrate that the same thinking applies across domains.

That creates a broader identity:

> **Decision thinking powered by data, evidence and experimentation.**

Only after the breadth has been demonstrated should the project concentrate on domains where there is a strong combination of:

- expertise
- experience
- interest
- audience pull
- practical potential

## Authority Model

FDTD intentionally distinguishes three voices.

### Expert Voice
Used when there is substantial professional experience.

### Practitioner Voice
Used when the subject comes from personal experience and experimentation.

### Learner Voice
Used when exploring a new field.

Being explicit about which voice is being used is part of the project's credibility.

## The Real-World Test

A useful FDTD question is:

> **What changes when this idea meets reality?**

This naturally creates an Academia ↔ Industry bridge for Data/AI topics, while also allowing the same approach to be used for investing, health, parenting, farming and life decisions.

## Learning Loop

Experience

   ↓
