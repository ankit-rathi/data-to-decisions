# Academia–Industry Gaps

This is the primary topic-selection filter.

Ask:

> Is there a meaningful gap between what academia teaches and what industry actually requires?

| Topic | Academic importance | Industry importance | What academia teaches | What industry needs | Gap | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Gap Categories

Possible sources of the gap:

- Scale
- Messy data
- Distributed systems
- Reliability
- Security
- Cost
- Legacy systems
- Regulation
- Organizational constraints
- Human behaviour
- Changing requirements
- Operational complexity
- Business decision-making
