# Content Flywheel

> **One piece of thinking → one sketch → multiple expressions → feedback → next question.**

## The Minimum Flywheel

```text
REAL-WORLD QUESTION
        ↓
RESEARCH / EXPERIENCE
        ↓
UNDERSTAND
        ↓
ONE KEY INSIGHT
        ↓
ONE HANDWRITTEN SKETCH
        ↓
SHORT / REEL
        ↓
OPTIONAL LINKEDIN / BLOG / YOUTUBE
        ↓
FEEDBACK
        ↓
NEXT QUESTION
        ↺
```

## Start With a Question

Good questions come from:

- professional experience
- personal experiments
- contradictions
- commonly misunderstood ideas
- academic concepts
- things being built
- decisions currently being made

Examples:

- Why can accurate data still produce a bad decision?
- Is diversification actually reducing risk?
- Should you leave a stable career because you are unhappy?
- Is organic farming economically viable?
- Why does an AI proof-of-concept fail in production?

## Research / Experience

Use research when the topic requires external evidence.

Use direct experience when the topic is a personal experiment.

Use both when appropriate.

Capture only what is needed to understand the question.

## The One-Insight Rule

Before creating content, finish this sentence:

> **"If the audience remembers only one thing, it should be..."**

That becomes the centre of the sketch and Short.

## Sketch

The sketch is the primary visual thinking artifact.

It should show:

- problem
- relationships
- contrast
- mental model
- key insight

It does not need to be artistically perfect.

## Short

A Short should communicate **one strong idea**, not the entire research effort.

Example:

> "Your data can be 100% accurate and still be useless."

Then explain the missing context.

## Deeper Formats

Only create a blog, long YouTube video or multi-part series when the idea earns it through:

- genuine importance
- audience pull
- personal expertise
- sustained curiosity

## Feedback

Capture useful signals:

- retention
- saves
- shares
- comments
- questions
- repeat requests
- follower quality
- your own interest

Views are one signal, not the decision.

## Next Question

A strong topic should naturally create another question.

```text
Data Quality
   ↓
Is quality context-dependent?
   ↓
What is decision fitness?
   ↓
Who owns the decision?
   ↓
How should the data product change?
```

That is the flywheel.
