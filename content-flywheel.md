# Content Flywheel

## The Principle

One question.

One deep research effort.

Multiple expressions.

---

## The Flywheel

Real-world question
        ↓
Research
        ↓
Understand
        ↓
Synthesise
        ↓
Sketch
        ↓
Blog
        ↓
LinkedIn
        ↓
YouTube
        ↓
Short / Reel
        ↓
Audience feedback
        ↓
New questions
        ↺

---

## Step 1 — Question

Start with something genuinely interesting.

Examples:

- Is more data always better?
- What does diversification actually mean?
- Why do ML models fail in production?
- Is career growth similar to portfolio management?

Avoid starting with:

> "I need to make a post today."

---

## Step 2 — Research

Understand:

- concept
- theory
- evidence
- practical application
- limitations
- disagreements
- industry reality

Use primary/high-quality sources wherever practical.

---

## Step 3 — Understand

Before creating content, be able to answer:

1. What is the concept?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What is commonly misunderstood?
4. What happens in practice?
5. What is my key insight?

---

## Step 4 — Sketch

Create one handwritten English sketch.

The sketch should capture the mental model.

It should be understandable without the accompanying video.

---

## Step 5 — Blog

The blog is the deepest public explanation.

Possible structure:

Problem
→ Concept
→ Evidence
→ Industry reality
→ Mental model
→ Practical implications
→ Conclusion

---

## Step 6 — LinkedIn

Compress the idea.

Focus on:

Hook
→ Problem
→ Insight
→ Implication

---

## Step 7 — YouTube

Explain the idea conversationally in Hindi/Hinglish.

Use the sketch as the visual anchor.

---

## Step 8 — Short/Reel

Extract one strong idea from the longer explanation.

Do not attempt to explain the entire topic.

---

## Language

Hindi/Hinglish:

- YouTube
- Shorts
- Instagram Reels

English:

- Sketches
- Blog
- LinkedIn
- GitHub
- Quantvesting product/documentation

---

## The Rule

Do not create five separate pieces of content.

Create one piece of thinking and distribute it five ways.
