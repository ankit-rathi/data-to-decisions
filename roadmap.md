# Roadmap

## Guiding Strategy

> **Breadth → Evidence → Depth → Build → Reassess**

The roadmap is intentionally adaptive.

---

# Phase 1 — BREADTH
## Sep 2026 → roughly first 30 topics

Objective:

> Establish what From Data To Decisions means and demonstrate its applicability beyond one domain.

Target:

**~30 sketch notes + ~30 Shorts**

Suggested breadth:

- Overview / decision thinking
- Wealth / financial planning
- Investing / Quantvesting
- Data & AI in industry
- Health & fitness
- Career
- Family / parenting
- Life decisions
- Organic farming

Do not try to become an expert in all of them.

---

# Phase 2 — VALIDATE
## After the first ~30

Review each domain against:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Audience pull | Did people care? |
| Personal pull | Did I enjoy exploring it? |
| Experience | Can I contribute something genuine? |
| Expertise | Can I go deeper credibly? |
| Potential | Is there a meaningful long-term direction? |

Classify each domain:

**DOUBLE DOWN / DEVELOP / MAINTAIN / DROP**

This is a decision gate, not a permanent label.

---

# Phase 3 — DEPTH
## Approximately 2027–2028

Choose 2–3 primary areas for deeper series.

Likely candidates include:

### Data & AI in the Real World
Academia ↔ Industry, architecture, data products, governance, GenAI, AI agents.

### Quantvesting
Financial decision-making, quantitative methods, portfolio analytics and the actual system being built.

### One or more emerging areas
Selected from audience + experience + interest.

The remaining domains continue as occasional breadth content.

---

# Phase 4 — BUILD
## Approximately 2028–2029

Where there is sustained evidence, turn knowledge into practical artefacts:

- frameworks
- notebooks
- demonstrations
- tools
- case studies
- educational resources
- advisory capability where appropriate

Quantvesting can become the strongest living build example.

---

# Phase 5 — CHOOSE
## Approximately 2029–2030

Use the accumulated evidence to decide where to spend more of your time.

Possible combination:

```text
Capital
  +
Data / AI education & advisory
  +
Quantvesting
  +
Agriculture / lifestyle
```

There is no requirement that all components become businesses.

The objective is **optionality**.

---

# The 2030 Gate

2030 should be treated as a **decision gate**, not a mandatory resignation date.

Before making a major career transition, evaluate:

- financial runway
- family readiness
- professional reputation
- content traction
- advisory potential
- investment track record
- farming evidence
- preferred location
- personal energy / meaning

If the evidence is insufficient, extend the corporate runway.

---

# Long-Term Balance

After the exploratory period, maintain a balance between:

### THINK
Research, theory, frameworks.

### BRIDGE
Connect theory with real-world practice.

### BUILD
Create practical systems and experiments.

The balance should evolve with evidence.
